BAY 4 · PRIVACY

Last updated October 2026.

What Bay 4 collects

Nothing. Bay 4 has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It never sends data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress (cleared blueprints, stars, seals, badges, memos read) and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional Ring Sector unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Bay 4 only learns whether the unlock is owned.

Children

Bay 4 collects no information from anyone, including children.

Dialed In

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What Dialed In collects

Nothing. Dialed In has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives any data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your courses, cards, imported documents, study progress and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your courses, cards and study progress sync between your devices through your own private iCloud storage (Apple's CloudKit private database). Only your Apple ID can read it; the developer can't access it. Pictures and imported documents stay on the device that imported them. You can turn sync off in Settings, or manage the data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

Widgets and notifications

Widgets, Live Activities and study reminders are created on your device from data already on it. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

AI on your device by default

By default, questions are written and checked by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, and the text you import never leaves it.

Your own API key (optional)

If you add your own Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key, the text you choose to import, and the cards being checked or explained, are sent to that provider to process your request, under their terms and privacy policy. The developer never sees them. Your key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is never included in backups or exports. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Sharing

When you share a set as a link, the set is encoded inside the link after the #, which browsers do not send to the website's server. Files you share go wherever you send them.

Purchases

The optional Dialed In Pro unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Dialed In only learns whether you own the unlock.

Children

Dialed In collects no information from anyone, including children.

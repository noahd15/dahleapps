What to Wear

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What What to Wear collects

Nothing. What to Wear has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives any data from the app.

Your location

To get the forecast, the app sends your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to the weather service: Apple Weather (WeatherKit), or Open-Meteo if Apple Weather is unavailable. It goes nowhere else. If you search for a city instead, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find it. Your last location is stored on your device so the forecast can refresh in the background.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your clothes list and your "too cold / just right / too warm" check-ins are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications and widgets

The morning notification and widgets are created on your device from the forecast. Notifications are scheduled locally; nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Children

What to Wear collects no information from anyone, including children.

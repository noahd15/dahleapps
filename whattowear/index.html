What to Wear

Support

What to Wear checks the forecast for your day and tells you what to wear each morning: the layer, what goes with it, and whether to bring an umbrella or a jacket for later.

Contact

Bugs or questions: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

The morning notification

The app schedules tomorrow's note whenever it gets a fresh forecast. Opening the app or having the widget on your home screen keeps it current. If the forecast is old by morning, the note is a little more general.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What Find a Set! collects

Nothing. Find a Set! has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives any data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, streaks, statistics and settings) is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Find a Set! reports achievements and leaderboard scores to it. That information is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out of Game Center in Settings at any time.

Multiplayer

Nearby games send moves directly between the phones in the game over your local network. Online games send moves between players through Game Center. Nothing about a game is stored after it ends, and none of it reaches the developer.

Purchases

Find a Set! is free, with no in-app purchases.

Children

Find a Set! collects no information from anyone, including children.

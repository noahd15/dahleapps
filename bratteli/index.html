Basic construction

Bratteli diagrams for A ⊂ B ⊂ ⟨B, eA⟩

Give a unital inclusion of finite-dimensional C*-algebras A ⊂ B and its inclusion matrix G. The page draws the Bratteli diagram of A ⊂ B ⊂ B1, where B1 = ⟨B, eA⟩ is the Jones basic construction, and can keep iterating the tower.

How it works

Write A = ⊕i Mai and B = ⊕j Mbj, and let Gij be the number of times the i-th summand of A sits inside the j-th summand of B. The inclusion is unital exactly when b = Gᵀa, which the page checks.

The basic construction B1 = ⟨B, eA⟩ ≅ EndA(B) is Morita equivalent to A, so its summands are indexed by the summands of A. The inclusion B ⊂ B1 has matrix Gᵀ: the Bratteli diagram of B ⊂ B1 is the reflection of the diagram of A ⊂ B. The sizes of B1 are c = Gb, so ci = Σj Gij bj.

Iterating, Bk+1 = ⟨Bk, ek⟩ and the inclusion matrices alternate G, Gᵀ, G, … When the diagram of A ⊂ B is connected, the Markov trace is unique and the Jones index is [B : A] = ‖G‖², the square of the largest singular value of G. The same index holds at every step of the tower.

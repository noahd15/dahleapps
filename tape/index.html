Tape

Support

Tape is a course in the theory of computation, with lessons and practice built like puzzles: automata, lambda calculus, decidability and complexity.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a puzzle you think is wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

Restoring a purchase

Bought the full course on another device? Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Game Center.

Privacy

Tape collects nothing. Privacy policy

Dialed In

AI study cards · Support

In Dialed In, one AI turns your slides, notes and practice exams into study cards, a second AI checks the answers, and your reviews are spaced so everything comes up before the exam. Make sets by hand or import them from Quizlet, too.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a card the checker got wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

AI on your iPhone, or with your own key

By default the questions are written and checked by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it. You can instead add your own Claude or OpenAI API key in Settings › AI engine; then the text you import, and the cards being checked, are sent to that provider. Your key is stored in the iPhone's Keychain only.

Sharing sets

Share a set as a link or a file. A link carries the set inside it, after the #, so it never reaches this website's server. Anyone without the app can still browse the cards in a browser.

Dialed In Pro

One purchase. No subscription. No ads. Pro adds AI card writing and checking; every study mode, sets, import, export and backup are free. Bought it on another device? Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

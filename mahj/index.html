Mahj with Friends

American mahjong · Support

Mahj with Friends is American mahjong for your phone: the Charleston, calls, jokers and a hand card, played against computer players or with friends at the same table or far away.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a hand that scored wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

The card

The app comes with its own 2026 card, written for this app. It isn't an official yearly card. With Mahj Pro, you can play with a card you own: add its hands under Your cards, by typing them or taking a photo of the card. Cards you add stay on your phone, and you can share them with friends as a link. At a friend's table you play the card they picked, with or without Pro.

Playing with friends

At the same table, phones find each other nearby over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with no account. From anywhere, invite friends through Game Center. Empty seats are filled by computer players.

Price

Free to play, including every game with friends, Easy and Medium computer players, hints and undo. Mahj Pro is a one-time $4.99 purchase that adds your own cards, the Hard computer, stats and history, and more tile sets and table colors. It works with Family Sharing; to restore it on a new phone, open Settings, then Mahj Pro, then Restore Purchases. Tips in the tip jar are optional and unlock nothing. No ads and no subscription.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Privacy policy

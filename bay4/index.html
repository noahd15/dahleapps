Bay 4

Support

Bay 4 is a puzzle game about inspecting circuit blueprints: untangle the ones that can be built, and prove the ones that can't.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a blueprint you think is wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

Restoring the Ring Sector

Bought the Ring Sector on another device? Open the Ring Sector screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Game Center.

Privacy

Bay 4 collects nothing. Privacy policy

Sulks

Support

Sulks is a puzzle game about critters who can't stand each other: keep rivals apart, lead everyone around one circle, and more.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a puzzle you think is wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

Price

Free to download, with no ads and no subscription. Rooms, Ring, Lookouts and the Daily puzzle cost nothing, and every Daily board stays free. Two optional one-time purchases are shared with your family: the expansion (Expert Lookouts, Numbers and Echoes, $2.99) and the critter looks ($1.99). Tips are optional thank-yous and unlock nothing.

Restoring a purchase

Bought the expansion or critter looks on another device? Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Game Center.

Privacy

Sulks collects nothing. Privacy policy

Bay 4

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What Bay 4 collects

Nothing. Bay 4 has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives any data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress (cleared blueprints, stars, seals, badges, memos read) and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Bay 4 reports achievements and leaderboard scores to it. That information is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out of Game Center in Settings at any time.

Purchases

The optional Ring Sector unlock and tips are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Bay 4 only learns whether you own the unlock.

Children

Bay 4 collects no information from anyone, including children.

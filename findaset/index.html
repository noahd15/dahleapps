Find a Set!

Support

Find a Set! is an unofficial app for playing SET, the card game designed by Marsha Falco: spot trios of cards that are all the same or all different in each feature.

Contact

Bugs, questions or a puzzle you think is wrong: dahle.apps@gmail.com. A screenshot sent from TestFlight works too.

Price

Free, with no in-app purchases, no ads and no subscription.

Playing with friends

Nearby games connect phones in the same room directly, so Find a Set! asks for Local Network access the first time. Online games use Game Center: host a game and share the 4-digit code or link, or join with a code. Everyone needs the same version of the app.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Game Center.

Privacy

Find a Set! collects nothing. Privacy policy
